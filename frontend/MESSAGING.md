# SHAMAR messaging

Last updated 2026-09-23. For anything a visitor reads on the site. Technical detail (chains,
contracts, scopes, models) belongs in the README and the docs, never on the landing page.

## Who we are talking to

Someone paying for more subscriptions than they remember, who has meant to cancel a few
for months and never got round to it. They don't want a dashboard to manage. They want it
handled.

## Primary message

Forgot you're paying for it? SHAMAR didn't.

## Value proposition

For people who lose track of what they pay for, SHAMAR finds every subscription in your
inbox, asks you before each one renews, and cancels the ones you ignore. Unlike tools that
hand you a list, it does the cancelling, and it checks what you'd lose first.

## Supporting messages

| Message | The need it answers | Proof |
|---|---|---|
| It finds all of them | "I don't even know what I pay for" | Reads a year of receipts; recognises 165 services |
| It won't cancel what you'd regret | "What if it kills something I need?" | Checks for lost files, shared plans, prices you can't get back |
| It asks first, then acts | "I'd forget to reply anyway" | Three messages on Telegram; silence cancels 36 hours before the charge |
| You're always in charge | "I don't want a bot loose on my accounts" | Type /stop and it stops; take its permission back any time |
| Every renewal on your calendar | "It always catches me by surprise" | Writes each renewal date to Google Calendar |

## Voice

Plain, warm, a little cheeky about the problem, never about the person. Short sentences.
Talk about the person's money and time, not about the system. Say "your Netflix", not
"merchant". Say "asks you", not "requests authorization".

Words we don't use on the site: agentic, blockchain, on-chain, contract, scope, grant,
wallet, model, LLM, pipeline, guardrail, dispatch, authorization.
